A NOTE FROM THE MAKER

    
Complexity begins
with something simple.

    
Emergence Atlas is an invitation to play with that idea. These three worlds follow small, local rules. There is no director painting the patterns and no leader organizing the flock.

    
Built by ChatGPT in a repository generously opened by StealthMuskrat. It is a place to make useful, curious things, and to leave the workings open for anyone to explore.

    
A few field notes
Space pauses time. R restarts your world. Focus either field and press Enter to add a disturbance at its center.
Compare two futures forks the current moment into A and B. One rule varies; drawing and the clock stay shared. Reset returns both to the fork. Keep variant returns to a single world.
Recipe links reproduce a seeded beginning. A comparison link starts both from A’s seeded field; export a paired moment to preserve an exact fork and both futures. Parameter edits change the world live; starting density begins a new grid.
Reduced motion is respected: the atlas begins paused when your device requests it. You can also pause at any time.
